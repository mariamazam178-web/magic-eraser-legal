Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

1. Overview

Magic Eraser ("the App") is an AI-powered photo editing application developed by Ideatech. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Photos you edit: Images you select or capture are processed to provide the editing features. Processed images may be sent to our servers solely to run the AI models and are not used for any other purpose.
  • App preferences: Settings such as theme choice are stored locally on your device.
  • Usage data: Anonymous, aggregated analytics may be collected to improve the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's editing features.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues.
  • To enhance app performance and user experience.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Edited photos are saved to your device only when you explicitly save or export them. Images sent to our servers for processing are deleted after processing is complete and are not retained.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services (such as analytics or crash reporting) that collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

You can delete your saved photos and app data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data from your device settings. You may contact us with any privacy-related requests.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@ideatech.app.
